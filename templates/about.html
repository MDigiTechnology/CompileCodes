Why use an online compiler?

Online IDEs help students, interview candidates, and developers test snippets quickly without installing toolchains. Search engines rank useful tools higher when pages load fast, work on mobile, and explain clearly what the tool does — that is why we publish dedicated pages for each language and maintain privacy and terms pages for transparency.
